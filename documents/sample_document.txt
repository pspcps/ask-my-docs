# Sample Company Documentation

## Employee Handbook

### Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to the following leave benefits:

1. **Annual Leave**: 20 working days per year
2. **Sick Leave**: 10 working days per year
3. **Personal Leave**: 5 working days per year

#### How to Apply for Leave

1. Submit leave request through the HR portal
2. Provide at least 2 weeks notice for annual leave
3. Emergency leave can be requested with manager approval
4. All leave must be approved by your direct supervisor

### Refund Process

Our company offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all products and services.

#### Refund Eligibility

- Product must be in original condition
- Return within 30 days of purchase
- Original receipt required
- Shipping costs are non-refundable

#### Refund Steps

1. Contact customer service within 30 days
2. Provide order number and reason for return
3. Receive return authorization number
4. Ship item back with RMA number
5. Refund processed within 5-7 business days
